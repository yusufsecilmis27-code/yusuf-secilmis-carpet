Frequently asked questions

Do you have a minimum order quantity?

No. You can order a single bale or a mixed-model container.

How do I get prices?

We do not list prices on the site. Tap the WhatsApp button on a product page; the product code is added to the message and we send the current wholesale price.

Can I combine different product groups in one order?

Yes. You can combine products from different categories in one order and one shipment.

Which languages can I use?

We reply directly in Turkish, English and Arabic, and help in other languages with translation support.
